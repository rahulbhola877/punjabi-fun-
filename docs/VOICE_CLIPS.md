# Voice clips — what each says, who says it, where it plays

All files are in `public/audio/voice/`. Text is the script each take was generated from (ElevenLabs eleven_v4);
the files were checked against the original takes by waveform, so names match content.
"Voice" is the ElevenLabs library voice used (several characters share one).

**E** young man (lafanga) · **F** loud man (angry neighbour) · **G** old man (Ustad)

## Characters
**Ranggarh bazaar (start):** Bhola Singh (dhaba owner), Kaku the cook, Gurnam Kaur (mechanic, Gurnam Motor Works),
Pammi (fruit & sabzi stall), Masterji (tailor), Sohal (cycle store), Mrs. Kaur (elderly neighbour, courtyard),
Gill uncle, Dr. Sehgal (Sehat Chemist), shopkeepers (Mithi Hatti, Kapda Ghar, Lali General Store, Dhillon Hardware,
Ranggarh Mobile), diners, chatting neighbours, kids, walkers, two beat constables.

**Ludhiana / Chaura Bazaar:** Pinka (Hello Mobile Zone), sabzi-walas, doodh-wala, kabadi-wala, auto-walas,
Conductor at the bus adda, Havaldar ji and constables, shoppers (shehri), aunties.

**Nawan Shehar (city):** Conductor Bittu (bus adda), Munshi Ji (anaj mandi), Havaldar Balwant (police chowki),
Ticket-wala Raju (Raj Talkies), lafangas, rehri vendors.

**Highway & villages:** Sher Singh and Sukhi Bhaji (Sher-e-Punjab dhaba), truckers and cleaners, pump attendant,
naka constables, Sarpanch Hardev Singh and villagers (Pind Kotli), Ustad Kartar Pehlwan (akhada), Jarnail Singh (dera),
kisaan, kabaddi players.

## To change a mapping
Tell Claude e.g. "use clip 30 for Gill uncle" or "clip 26 should be the bus conductor". Named-character lines live in
`NAMED_LINES` / `GREETS` in `src/crowds/Barkers.ts`; street calls are `callClip` in `src/crowds/countryFolk.ts`;
mission lines in `src/missions/Missions.ts`; fights/police in `src/combat/*.ts`.
